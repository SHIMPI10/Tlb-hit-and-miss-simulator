How to read the results

TLB hit: the VPN is already cached, so the stored PFN is reused.

TLB miss: the simulator performs a page-table walk, derives PFN = 1000 + VPN, and inserts the mapping. If the TLB is full, FIFO removes the oldest inserted entry and LRU removes the least recently used entry.

EMAT: (hits × hit time + misses × miss time) ÷ total references.
